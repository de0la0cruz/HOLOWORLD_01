# Peer Review — HOLOWORLD v1

This branch opens HOLOWORLD for peer review. Reviewers: read, challenge, and comment — corrections land as commits on this branch; accepted changes merge to `master`.

## What is under review

- `PHILOSOPHY.md` — the portrait: ethos, vibe, personality, philosophy, glossary
- `SPECS.md` — the build specs: contents, the rhizome method, rebuild steps
- `rhizome.html` + `graph.json` — the knowledge graph: 19 nodes, 30 edges

## Review questions

1. Does the graph match the philosophy? Are nodes or edges missing that the texts support — or present that they don't?
2. Is anything stated as source that is actually inference or coinage? Known flags: HALOGOD is the author's coinage (not in the source texts); the v2 semantic pass was curated, with Graphify 0.9.91 handling structure/export.
3. Can a stranger rebuild or extend this from `SPECS.md` alone, without the author? Where does the spec fail them?
4. Continuity: do the five threads (holoworld, hologenesis, halo, halogod, transcendent encryption) hold together as one system?

## Ground rules

Receipts over vibes: cite the file and line you are challenging. Coinage, inference, and source must stay labeled as such. MIT — fork freely, argue freely.
